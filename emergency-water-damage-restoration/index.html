Maintenance Tips for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Shut off the water at the main whenever you leave home for more than a few days.
  • Important paperwork and valuables belong above floor level, most of all on the lowest story.
  • A sump pump should be tried out before storms arrive, ideally with a backup battery installed.
  • Place leak sensors near the water heater, washing machine, dishwasher and under sinks.
  • Every member of the household should know where the main water valve is and how it closes.
  • Being able to find the electrical panel quickly means wet areas can be de-energized without risk.

When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Union City

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water is still flowing and you cannot find or reach the shutoff valve.
  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • You came home to water and do not know how long it ran.
  • Water has reached the electrical panel, appliances or power strips on the floor.
  • A leak upstairs is sending water down the walls of the level beneath it.
  • Sewage has backed up, or the water is dark with a foul odor.

The Benefits of Professional Emergency Water Damage Restoration

The visible water is the easy part of an emergency. The difficulty lies in tracking where the remaining water travelled. Within minutes it slips under baseboards, inside wall cavities and below the floor. Our night crew arrives with gear homes simply do not have, including commercial air movers, properly sized dehumidifiers and truck-mounted extractors. Our Emergency Water Damage Restoration Process

We follow a proven process to ensure every job is completed to the highest standards. Here's how we handle emergency water damage restoration from start to finish:

1

Answer the Call

A person answers at any hour, asks about the source, the rooms affected and any safety concerns, and tells you what to do while a crew is on the way to your home.

2

Stop the Source

First we rule out shock and contamination dangers, and then whatever feeds the water gets shut down or isolated. Until the flow ends, no other step does much good.

3

Remove Standing Water

Standing water comes out by pump and truck-mounted extraction, furniture is shifted to dry ground, and thermal imaging plus meters reveal the moisture inside walls and under floors.

4

Set Equipment and Plan

The crew does not go until the drying equipment, meaning air movers and dehumidifiers, is running. We then walk you through what we found, what the next 3 to 5 days of drying look like, and what your insurer will need.
